## Notes

- **Stand-in vitest config:** upstream's `packages/prompts/vitest.config.ts` is unchanged at 1.9.0; the pinned hash still matches, nothing to reconcile.
- **`guide` suite: skipped.** The Recorder harvests it fine (each case records 12 prompts in one Scenario), but the Rust loader drops every multi-prompt Scenario (`tests/scenarios/mod.rs`, `runs.len() != 1`), so `guide.json` would load as zero Scenarios. Splitting multi-prompt Scenarios would also split `group()` flows, which share one output. Every claim it makes (gray guide bar, none under `withGuide: false` local or global) is already covered by the per-prompt suites' `withGuide` cases.
- **Accessible scripted cases:** 8 cases in `scripts/scripted/cases.mjs` (7 spinner, 1 progress). In accessible mode `delay` drives nothing, so they set `delay: 30_000` and one `tick` is one heartbeat; no new step op. Abort-signal, `ACCESSIBLE`-env and `accessible: false`-over-global-setting cases are about how the setting resolves, not what is drawn, and are not expressible as a script of calls and are left to ticket 09's unit tests. Recorded bytes match upstream's own `spinner-accessible` expectations; the accessible progress bar's heartbeat carries the bar's ANSI, which is upstream behaviour.
